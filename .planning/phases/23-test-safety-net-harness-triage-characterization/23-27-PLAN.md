## Harness Gap Procedure

This applies from the end of this plan until the phase ends, to every plan that uses `test/realpath/harness/` without owning it. Every harness capability a triage or scenario plan names is specified in plans 23-01, 23-28, 23-02 or 23-27, or by 23-20 for `serializer.ts`. That was checked on 2026-11-09 by reading every task in plans 23-05 to 23-24 against those plans, and the check added the alert list (`recordedAlerts`), the fee setter with its legacy mode (`setFees`), logs stored in blocks for `eth_getLogs` (`mine({ logs })` and `emitLogs`), receipt logs (`setReceiptLogs`), `params` and `data` on `callLog`, the `at` time on timeline entries, `timeline` on a scenario, and subscriptions over a real loopback socket (`serveLoopback`). So this procedure is for what could not be foreseen.

1. **Raise.** The plan's executor stops the task, writes nothing under `test/realpath/harness/`, leaves its completed tasks committed, and returns a `checkpoint:decision` with no `auto_select`, titled `HARNESS GAP`, so auto mode still stops at it. The checkpoint names what is missing, the plan and task waiting on it, why a fixture beside that plan's tests cannot supply it, and quotes this section in full. In plain words it offers the operator three options:
   - `allow-harness-change`, "Yes, change the shared test tools." Steps 2 to 6 run.
   - `decline-harness-change`, "No, leave the shared test tools as they are." Nothing under `test/realpath/harness/` changes and steps 2 to 5 do not run. If the waiting task is a triage task, its entry is recorded with `set-triage.ts --outcome unreproduced`, with the claims the test could prove and the claim left open named in the note. It stays open for the operator's review in plan 23-16 and never defaults to NOT-A-BUG (AC-08). The plan goes on to its other tasks. If the waiting task is not a triage task, what it delivers stays undelivered: the plan stops at that task, its summary names the acceptance criterion left unmet, and the orchestrator reports the phase as blocked on it. No acceptance criterion, finding or requirement is dropped.
   - `stop-phase`, "Stop here and come back to it." No plan is dispatched, and the plan stays paused at this checkpoint until the operator resumes it.
2. **Pause.** On that checkpoint the execute-phase orchestrator dispatches no new plan. Plans already running finish or reach their own checkpoint before step 3 starts.
