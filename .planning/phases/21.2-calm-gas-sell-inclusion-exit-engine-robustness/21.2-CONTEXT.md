## Implementation Decisions

### D-QUALITY-FIRST [USER-BINDING] — governs every decision below
At every implementation/design choice in this phase (and downstream planning +
execution), pick the option that is **BETTER and SAFER and produces
HIGHER-QUALITY RESULTS, regardless of code complexity or LOC.** "Minimal surface /
least code / simplest" is NOT a virtue and must not be used as a tiebreaker.
Carried verbatim from Phase 18/19/20/21.1. Capital safety + outcome quality come
first. Every decision below was made under this lens (the owner repeatedly chose
the safer/higher-assurance option over the smaller one).

### Finding A — Calm-gas exit inclusion
- **D-EXIT-INCLUSION (layered):** an exit that can't get included at calm gas
  lands via a **two-stage ladder**: (1) **escalate the sell privately** with an
  **independent, higher sell-leg priority-fee ceiling** (decoupled from the buy
  ceiling), optionally augmented with a coinbase bribe; (2) if still not included,
  **fall back to a direct public-mempool sell** — promote the
  `scripts/recover-sell-ladys.ts` path (`swapExactTokensForETHSupportingFeeOnTransferTokens`,
  fee-on-transfer-safe) into the engine. Rationale (D-QUALITY-FIRST): MEV
  protection when it works, **guaranteed exit when it doesn't** — for an exit
  (esp. anti-rug/stop-loss), a stranded position is the worse outcome than a
  sandwiched sell. Chosen over public-only (gives up protection too early) and
  private-only (strands the position on a thin wallet — the exact failure).
- **D-FALLBACK-TRIGGER (reason-aware / urgency-driven):** the switch from
  private-escalation to public fallback is **gated by the exit reason** (already
  passed to `executeFullSell`): **anti-rug exits go public FAST** (0–1 quick
  private attempts — the rug is happening now); **routine exits**
  (trailing-stop / stop-loss / 30-min urgency) **escalate private for N attempts,
  then fall back.** Chosen over a fixed-N or pure time-budget trigger because a
  rug-in-progress must not retry private bundles while liquidity drains.
- **D-EXIT-AFFORDABILITY-GATE (hard entry gate):** at **buy time, refuse the
  snipe** unless the wallet can afford the **worst-case GUARANTEED exit** — the
  public-mempool sell gas + a buffer (the always-available path). Strengthen the
  affordability reserve already in `reconciliation.ts` (`reserveSellGas`) so its
  target is the *guaranteed* exit, not just the private ceiling. Defense-in-depth:
  **never enter a position you cannot exit.** Chosen over "public fallback is
  enough" / "operator warning only" per D-QUALITY-FIRST.
- **D-FALLBACK-NO-DOUBLE-SELL:** before the public-mempool sell submits, guard
  against double-selling (the in-flight private bundle could land late) and
  selling-nothing: **(1) re-run 21.1's inclusion reconciliation on the last
  private attempt (receipt + nonce) and ABORT the public sell if it actually
  landed; (2) re-fetch the on-chain token balance and sell only what is still
  held.** Directly reuses `D-RECONCILE-RECEIPT` + `D-NONCE-SAFE-RETRY` from 21.1.

### Finding D — Failed-exit position protection
- **D-FAILED-EXIT-KEEP-MONITORING:** **anti-rug monitoring stays registered until
  the position is genuinely closed** (confirmed sell / on-chain balance ~0) — NO
  MATTER how many exits fail. **Routine** exit auto-retries (stop-loss / trailing /
  urgency) are **bounded**: after **N consecutive failures, pause the ROUTINE
  retries + hard-alert the operator**, but **anti-rug-triggered emergency exits
  still fire** regardless. Protects against rug absolutely; bounds gas-burn on a
  stuck/sell-reverting position. This **corrects a misapplication of Phase 19's
  `D-TERMINATION`** (which always meant "unregister when the position *closes*";
  the bug wired the *failed-exit error path* to the same teardown — see
  `exitEngine.ts:561-573`, the `catch` that marks 'failed' + `deleteExitState` +
  `unsubscribeAntiRug` on a still-held position). NOT a reversal of D-TERMINATION.
- **D-BALANCE-IS-TRUTH:** after any exit attempt, the **on-chain `balanceOf` is
  the source of truth** for held-vs-closed: ~0 (dust threshold) → closed, tear
  down; > dust → still held, keep monitoring. This is Phase 21's `D-OP-DB-TRUTH`
  applied to the exit lifecycle — even when the submit-path report lies (the exact
  21.1/STOP-THE-LINE-02 false-negative class), the chain balance tells the truth
  (LADYS evidence: 252,930,777 confirmed held *after* the reported failure).
- **D-PERSIST-FAILED-HELD:** on a failed exit of a still-held position, **do NOT
  delete `exit_state`** — keep it so a restart **re-enters the position into
  monitoring + re-registers anti-rug**. Mark a **distinct status (e.g.
  `exit_failed_held`)** separate from `sold` and from `failed`(=dead), plus a
  **persisted failure counter** (drives the bounded-retry above). Makes the stuck
  state operator-visible on the dashboard. Builds on Phase 21 `D-23`/`EXIT-LIVE-02`
  exit-state restart persistence. Chosen over reusing `'open'` (less observable)
  and over the broader startup reconciliation sweep (deferred — see below).

### Finding C — Poll-loop decoupling + bot-wide nonce safety
- **D-DECOUPLE-SERIAL-WORKER:** **poll ticks must never block on a sell.** A tick
  that detects an exit trigger **enqueues** the exit and returns immediately
  (cadence preserved); a **single serial exit worker** drains the queue,
  submitting **one sell at a time** (serial submission is nonce-safe by
  construction for the single wallet). A **per-position lock** prevents the same
  position being enqueued/sold twice (no re-entrant self-sell). Chosen over
  async-per-exit (re-introduces cross-position nonce races) and over "keep it in
  the tick, just fix the blocking" (minimal-surface, not a D-QUALITY-FIRST
  tiebreaker; leaves the position's own next poll delayed).
- **D-NONCE-AUTHORITY-ALL:** route **EVERY signing transaction** — buys, exit
  sells, anti-rug sells, the public-mempool fallback, token approvals — through
  **one per-wallet serialization / nonce authority** so no two txs ever contend.
  Eliminates the nonce-collision (double-spend) class **bot-wide** and is the
  natural conclusion of 21.1's `D-SHARED-HELPER` centralization. Chosen over
  exit-family-only / exit-worker-only (both leave buy-vs-exit or anti-rug-vs-exit
  nonce races — the exact hazard) per D-QUALITY-FIRST.
- **D-EXIT-PRIORITY:** the serial worker processes queued exits by **urgency
  priority**: **anti-rug > 30-min urgency / stop-loss > trailing-stop >
  partial-sell.** In a rug or crash cascade, the exits protecting the most capital
  go first; a late-arriving anti-rug jumps ahead of a routine trailing stop.
  Reuses the same reason-aware urgency as `D-FALLBACK-TRIGGER`.

### Finding B — Freeze / kill-safety (defense-in-depth; root cause is a debug task)
- **D-DEFENSE-IN-DEPTH:** beyond the debugger's root-cause fix, add **all three
  layers**: (1) a **watchdog** that force-aborts any submit/cycle exceeding a hard
  wall-clock budget + alerts; (2) **hard SIGINT** (see below); (3) **timeouts on
  every provider/RPC call** so nothing hangs indefinitely. A funded live bot must
  be always killable and never silently wedged. Chosen over the two-layer and
  root-cause-only options per D-QUALITY-FIRST.
- **D-SIGINT-TWO-STAGE:** **first SIGINT begins graceful shutdown** (stop
  intervals, abort in-flight submit within a **bounded grace period**, flush
  state); **a second SIGINT OR the grace-timeout → force `process.exit()`
  immediately.** Never hangs; gives a clean shutdown a brief chance. Abandoning an
  in-flight submit is **safe** because `D-PERSIST-FAILED-HELD` (restart-resume) +
  `D-NONCE-AUTHORITY-ALL` make it recoverable on next boot.
- **D-WATCHDOG-ABORT:** on a budget overrun the watchdog **aborts just the stuck
  operation** (AbortController / timeout rejection), logs + Telegram-alerts, and
  **keeps the bot alive** monitoring everything else. The stuck position stays
  held + monitored per `D-FAILED-EXIT-KEEP-MONITORING` and is retried. Surgical —
  one wedged submit never takes down monitoring of other positions. Chosen over
  force-restart-process (needs a supervisor dependency) and alert-only (weakest;
  an event-loop freeze could block the alert itself).

<specifics>
## Specific Ideas

- **The exit leg is where reliable inclusion becomes capital-safety, not
  throughput** — the whole layered ladder (D-EXIT-INCLUSION) exists because a
  non-included *exit* strands a position; for an anti-rug exit that is the
  difference between recovering funds and a total rug loss. This is the same
  rationale 21.1's `D-FEE-ESCALATION` invoked, now proven necessary in the field.
- **On-chain balance is the only signal that didn't lie** — the LADYS retry showed
  the submit-path reporting failure while 252,930,777 LADYS sat confirmed
  on-chain. `D-BALANCE-IS-TRUTH` makes `balanceOf` authoritative for every
  held-vs-closed decision; the canonical regression test asserts monitoring
  survives a reported-failed-but-actually-held exit.
- **"Never hold what you can't exit"** — the owner chose a hard entry gate
  (D-EXIT-AFFORDABILITY-GATE) over softer warnings: the thin-wallet trap that
  produced Finding A should be impossible to enter, not just survivable.
- **The freeze is operationally unacceptable on a funded bot** — a process that
  ignores Ctrl+C while holding a position drove the full three-layer
  defense-in-depth posture (watchdog + hard SIGINT + RPC timeouts), independent of
  whatever root cause the debugger finds.

</specifics>
