<objective>
Wire all three MEV strategies through the shared `submitWithReconciliation` helper (D-SHARED-HELPER), eliminating the `baseNonce+attempt` loop and the premature `wait()===0`-only / relay-accept / `tx.wait`-timeout failure paths from flashbots, bloXroute, and passthrough. Each strategy becomes a thin wrapper that builds a per-strategy `submit` closure (simulate-before-submit + the native submit primitive) and delegates nonce-safety, inclusion-reconciliation, confirm-window, and fee-escalation to the helper. The `MevStrategy.sendTransaction` signature is UNCHANGED — the helper is an implementation detail behind it.

Then, per D-TEST-BOTH (unit half — the fork half lives in 21.1-03/21.1-06), flip the per-strategy `it.skip` stubs (created in 21.1-01) GREEN: misreport-but-landed → real hash + no second submit (the LADYS regression), mined-but-reverted → throw, nonce-advanced → stop, and the SC4 sell-leg inheritance.

This is the critical fix. It depends on the helper (21.1-02) and the stubs (21.1-01). It does NOT touch txManager/bot.ts/exitEngine — those (legacy deletion + hash recording) are plan 21.1-05.

Purpose: a buy/sell that actually lands can never again be reported as failed (the LADYS harm); the retry can never double-spend; a reverting tx is never escalated-and-resubmitted (D-SIMULATE-BEFORE-SUBMIT).
Output: 3 rewired strategy files; the per-strategy reconciliation tests green.
</objective>
