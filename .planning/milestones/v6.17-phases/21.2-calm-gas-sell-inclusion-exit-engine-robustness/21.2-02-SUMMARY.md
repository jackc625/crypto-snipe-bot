## Accomplishments
- `src/utils/withTimeout.ts` — deterministically bounds any long/hung provider call via `Promise.race([op(), timeoutReject])`, freeing the single Node event loop from a stalled RPC (threat T-21.2-HANG). The timeout timer is `.unref()`'d and cleared in `finally`, so it can never keep the process alive or leak (threat T-21.2-TIMER-LEAK), and the rejection is a real `Error` (never a bare string, the Flashbots `bundle.wait()` anti-pattern).
- 4 fake-timer tests prove real behavior: rejects after `ms` on a never-resolving op, passes through early resolution without a late reject, rejection is `instanceof Error`, and `vi.getTimerCount() === 0` after resolution (the `finally { clearTimeout }`).
- Registered the three Finding B liveness knobs in BOTH `config.ts` (`required:false` rows) and `.env.example` (default + 1-line description) per D-ENV-EXAMPLE, ready for plan 21.2-09 to consume without re-touching config.ts.

## Decisions Made
- **withTimeout frees, it does not cancel.** ethers v6 cannot reliably abort an in-flight `FetchRequest` (#4122/#4765), so the wrapper only stops the caller awaiting the hung op. Documented this explicitly inline so no caller assumes the network request is cancelled.
- **Global, not per-chain.** The three knobs are submission-mechanism/process-level liveness controls, so they sit with the Phase 21.1 MEV global knobs (no chain prefix), matching the established grouping.
- **Scope boundary honored.** Finding A (21.2-04) and Finding D (21.2-07) knobs were NOT added here, avoiding the cross-plan config.ts collision the plan called out.
