<objective>
Decouple sell submission from the per-position poll tick (Finding C, D-DECOUPLE-SERIAL-WORKER +
D-EXIT-PRIORITY). Today the 4-attempt reconciliation runs synchronously inside `pollExitStrategy`,
blocking the WHOLE poll cycle for ~222s (`exit.poll.cycle overruns:true`); with multiple open
positions, none are polled or exited during that window.

The fix: a poll tick that detects a trigger ENQUEUES `{tradeId, reason, priority}` and returns
`'continue'` IMMEDIATELY (cadence preserved); a single serial `ExitWorker` drains a priority queue,
submitting ONE sell at a time (serial = nonce-safe by construction for the single wallet); a
per-position lock (extending the existing `exitManager.ts` sell-lock Map) prevents re-entrant
self-sell. Priority: anti-rug(0) > urgency-market-sell(1) = stop-loss(1) > trailing-stop(2) >
partial(3) — a late-arriving anti-rug jumps ahead of a queued trailing stop.

This plan ships the worker + queue + poll-tick conversion. It does NOT yet change WHAT
`executeFullSell` does on submit (the inclusion ladder is 21.2-06, the balance-truth catch is
21.2-07) — those build on this same file, sequenced after. The worker drain calls the EXISTING
`executeFullSell`; wrap that call in `withNonceLock(ctx.wallet.address, ...)` (from 21.2-01) so the
serial worker is also nonce-serialized at the authority layer.

Purpose: one exit's retries never starve other positions' polling.
Output: ExitWorker + priority queue + per-position lock + poll-tick enqueue + tests proving overruns:false.
