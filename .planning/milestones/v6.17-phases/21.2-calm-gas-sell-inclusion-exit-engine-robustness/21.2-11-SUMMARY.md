## The Exact src/ Changes

**db.ts — the new statements (read side that was missing):**
- `getResumeExitMonitorTrades`: `SELECT DISTINCT t.token, t.chain, t.pool_type, t.fee_tier, t.id, COALESCE(e.exit_status = 'exit_failed_held', 0) AS is_failed_held FROM trades t LEFT JOIN exit_state e ON e.trade_id = t.id WHERE (t.status = 'pending' AND t.action = 'buy') OR e.exit_status = 'exit_failed_held'`
- `getResumeAntiRugTrades`: same union but `INNER JOIN lp_baseline b ON b.trade_id = t.id` (the `registerAntiRugPosition`/`loadLpBaseline` precondition) plus `b.pair_or_pool_address` in the projection.

**bot.ts — resumeMultiChainMonitoring (now exported):** drives off `getResumeExitMonitorTrades.all()` instead of the old inline `db.prepare('... WHERE status=? AND action=?').all('pending','buy')`. Logs `op:'exit.failed-held.resumed'` when `is_failed_held`. The existing `balance === 0n → updateTradeStatus('failed')` guard is unchanged and still wins (no resurrection).

**bot.ts — resumeAntiRugMonitoring:** Step 2 scan replaced the inline `db.prepare('... INNER JOIN lp_baseline ... WHERE t.status=pending ...')` with `getResumeAntiRugTrades.all()`; added a pre-loop check that warns (`op:'antirug.resume.failed-held-no-baseline'`) for any exit_failed_held id missing from the baseline-joined set; Step 3 logs `op:'exit.failed-held.resumed'` before re-registering a failed-held position.

## Decisions Made
- **Preserve the distinct lifecycle status (D-QUALITY-FIRST):** read the `exit_failed_held` marker on restart rather than collapsing the catch path to `'pending'`. A failed-still-held position must remain operator-visible as `failed`; the union re-enters it by its durable identity without erasing that signal.
- **Cached prepared statements in db.ts, not inline db.prepare in bot.ts:** higher quality per the WR-06 rationale (SQL errors at module load, one test surface). This is why the integration mocks needed updating — bot.ts now imports two new db symbols.
- **Single-query `is_failed_held` discriminator:** avoids a second query to drive the D-ALERTS log; `exit_state.trade_id` is a PK so the LEFT JOIN is one row per trade.
