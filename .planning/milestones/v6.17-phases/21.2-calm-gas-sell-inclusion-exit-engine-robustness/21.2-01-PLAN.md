<objective>
Create the per-wallet nonce-serialization authority `withNonceLock(walletAddress, op)` — the
spine of D-NONCE-AUTHORITY-ALL (Finding C). It is a per-wallet async promise-chain mutex: it
guarantees no two signing operations run concurrently for the same wallet, so no two ever
contend for a nonce. It COMPOSES WITH 21.1's re-fetch-'latest'-per-attempt scheme
(`reconciliation.ts:369-371`); the op inside the lock still re-fetches the nonce. It does NOT
replace that scheme and must NOT use ethers `NonceManager` (which predicts/caches nonces — the
opposite discipline).

inclusion ladder, 21.2-07 anti-rug sell, 21.2-08 buy entry path) wrap their submits in it.
