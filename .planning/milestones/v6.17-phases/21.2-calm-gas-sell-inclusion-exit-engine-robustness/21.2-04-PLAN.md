<objective>
Give the EXIT (sell) leg an INDEPENDENT priority-fee ceiling decoupled from the buy ceiling
(Finding A, D-EXIT-INCLUSION stage 1) and retarget the affordability reserve to the GUARANTEED
public exit (D-EXIT-AFFORDABILITY-GATE math). The Finding A root trap is that the sell currently
reuses `MEV_MAX_PRIORITY_FEE_GWEI` (5 gwei buy ceiling) → on a thin wallet at calm gas the clamp
capped the bid at ~12.5 gwei and the tiny no-MEV-value private bundle wasn't included.

CRITICAL invariant (Pitfall 5): the independent sell ceiling is "try harder privately," NEVER
"overspend." The affordability bound (`computeAffordablePriorityFee`'s `min(ceiling, affordabilityBound)`)
STILL clamps the final bid — the guaranteed inclusion lever is the public fallback (21.2-06), not an
over-bid private one. Separately, `reserveSellGas` must reserve the GUARANTEED public-exit gas
(a normal-priced tx) rather than the private CEILING priority fee (which over-reserves AND mis-targets).

This plan also OWNS the Finding A env registration (`MEV_SELL_MAX_PRIORITY_FEE_GWEI` ~25,
`MEV_SELL_COINBASE_BRIBE_*` default-OFF, the entry-gate `SELL_GAS_BASEFEE_BUFFER` / cushion knob)
in config.ts + .env.example, so 21.2-06 and 21.2-08 consume them without re-touching config.ts.

Purpose: a calm-gas thin-wallet exit can bid harder privately while the public fallback stays the guaranteed path.
